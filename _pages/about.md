Hello!

Ozzy is here...

I am a researcher who tries to apply cool Math formulas to real-life problems, and I like challenging myself with new technologies. My work sits on the security and efficiency of decentralised and collaborative AI — how to train, fine-tune and verify models across machines that do not trust each other.

<ul class="research-areas">
  <li>Decentralised training and inference</li>
  <li>Security and verifiability of machine learning</li>
  <li>Applied cryptography and blockchain</li>
</ul>

I am the Research Lead at [Gensyn](https://www.gensyn.ai), which I joined in 2023. My research there is on making decentralised training and inference hold up in practice: staying efficient when the machines are heterogeneous and unreliable, and staying secure when the participants cannot be assumed honest. Much of that is about verifiability: being able to check that a training run or an inference really happened as claimed.

Before Gensyn I was a postdoc at Radboud University (2022–23), working on the security of AI: robust collaborative machine learning, and in particular backdoor and poisoning attacks and the defences against them. I supervised PhD students and taught master's courses there. Earlier I was at TU Delft — a postdoc in 2021–22, and a graduate researcher from 2017 to 2021 — where I worked on the security, privacy, scalability and economic aspects of blockchain technology. Going from blockchain protocols to model training turned out to be a smaller jump than it sounds: both are about getting parties who do not trust each other to agree on what actually happened.

On the education side, I did my Ph.D. in the Cyber Security Group at TU Delft. Before Delft I was at Bogazici University in Istanbul, first for a double-major B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and Mathematics, then for an M.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering. That double major is probably where the habit of reaching for a Math formula first came from.

Personally, I would define myself as a sporty person. I like to try and fail at new things. I play amateur-level chess but also enjoy watching Super GM tournaments. To keep my creative side alive, I also paint once in a while.
